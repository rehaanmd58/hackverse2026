The Ambulance Bottleneck

CRITICAL SECONDS LOST IN DENSE TRAFFIC

In acute cardiac arrest, stroke, and traumatic bleeding, every sixty seconds lost reduces patient resuscitation survival by 7% to 10%. Emergency responders lose valuable minutes at uncoordinated city intersections where civilian traffic queues are compressed bumper-to-bumper.

The "Box Trap" Problem

When motorists wait at a red light, adjacent lanes are completely blocked. Drivers physically cannot pull aside, leaving ambulances completely trapped despite sirens blaring.

AVERAGE IMPACT: 4.5m–7.0m DELAY PER ROUTE
02

The Safety Paradox

THE HAZARDS OF ABRUPT SIGNAL DROPS

Ad-hoc intersection crossings are full of danger: emergency vehicles are 4.8 times more likely to be involved in a crash than regular traffic. When traffic signals change abruptly without clear warnings, secondary crashes frequently occur.

Panic Braking & Crosswalk Risks

Dropping cross-traffic lights suddenly causes panic stops and rear-end crashes. At the same time, pedestrians halfway across the street without a countdown buffer are left stranded and endangered.

SAFETY REQUIREMENT: 3.0s YELLOW + 2.0s BUFFER
03

The Municipal Solution

AUTOMATED PREEMPTION & SAFE CLEARANCE

Automated corridor clearance solves both problems. Emergency vehicles are detected 450 meters away, giving city signals ample time to safely transition conflicting traffic without alarming motorists.

Advance Queue Clearance

Crosswalks transition safely to solid don't-walk hands, cross-traffic slows down at standard yellow lights, and cars ahead of the ambulance are cleared before the unit even reaches the intersection.

PROVEN BENEFIT: 70%+ RESPONSE TIME SAVED
